Privacy Policy — Digital Cookie App

Effective date: 21 November 2026

App: Digital Cookie App (my.digitalcookie.dcfinance)

Developer: Digital Cookie, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Contact: system.digital.cookie@gmail.com

Digital Cookie App is a workspace companion that shows your inbox, tasks, meetings, finance and event data from services you connect. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

What the app handles

DataWhyWhere it goes
Account password and session token for your Digital Cookie serverTo sign you inSent to the server address you enter (default: our Reply Inbox server). The session token is kept on your phone.
Inbox, finance, events, bot-status, social and calendar dataTo display and let you act on it (approve a reply, mark a payment paid, add a receipt)Loaded from your Digital Cookie server and cached on your phone for offline use.
Notion account connection (OAuth access token, workspace name)To show and edit your Notion tasks and meeting notesThe sign-in happens on Notion's website. Our authentication service (dc-recorder-auth) exchanges the one-time code for a token; the token is stored on your phone and used to call the Notion API directly. We do not keep a copy of your Notion content.
Tasks and meeting notes you createCore featureWritten to your own Notion workspace.
Demo modeTry the app without an accountUses made-up sample data stored on your phone only. No request is made to any server.
Microphone audio (only when you tap Record meeting)To record a meetingStored on your phone, then uploaded to your Notion workspace to be transcribed by Notion. Recording runs with a visible notification.
Photos/receipts you choose to attach (camera or file picker)To attach a receipt to a finance entry or eventUploaded to your Digital Cookie server.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS, call log, location or other apps.

Third parties

  • Notion Labs, Inc. — receives the data you put in Notion. See notion.so/privacy.
  • Railway (hosting) — hosts our server and authentication service.

Storage, security and retention

All traffic uses HTTPS. Credentials and tokens are kept in app-private storage and excluded from Android backups. Cached data stays on your phone until you sign out or uninstall. Data on your Digital Cookie server and in your Notion workspace is kept until you delete it.

Your choices and deletion

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account / data signs you out and erases everything the app holds on your phone (caches, tasks, recordings, settings, tokens) and opens our deletion request page.
  • Sign out in Settings to remove the session and cached data from the phone; disconnect Notion in Notion settings, or revoke access in Notion → Settings → Connections.
  • To request deletion of data held on the Digital Cookie server, use the deletion request page or email system.digital.cookie@gmail.com; we will act within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
