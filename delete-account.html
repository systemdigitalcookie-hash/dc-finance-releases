Delete your account and data — Digital Cookie App

App: Digital Cookie App (my.digitalcookie.dcfinance) · Developer: Digital Cookie, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Delete the data on your phone (instant)

  1. Open the app → Settings → Delete account / data → Delete.
  2. This signs you out and erases everything stored on the phone: inbox and finance caches, tasks, recordings, settings, and the sign-in and Notion tokens.

Uninstalling the app does the same.

Ask us to delete data on the Digital Cookie server

If you signed in to a Digital Cookie server, your account and the data it holds there are deleted on request.

  1. Email system.digital.cookie@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Digital Cookie App data".
  2. Send it from the address linked to your account, and say which server you signed in to.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete the data within 30 days.

What gets deleted: your account and device sign-in tokens, and your inbox, finance, event and receipt data on the server. What we keep: nothing, except records we are legally required to keep.

Your Notion workspace

Tasks and meeting notes you created live in your own Notion workspace, not on our servers. To disconnect the app, open Notion → Settings → Connections and remove "Digital Cookie". Delete the pages in Notion if you no longer want them.

Demo mode

Demo mode uses made-up sample data stored only on your phone. Use Exit demo (or Delete account / data) to remove it.
